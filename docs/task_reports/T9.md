DEVIATIONS:
- The pendulum length is a MuJoCo slide joint held by a position servo (the fine-align style actuator). The period is the mean gap between positive-going zero crossings of the bob's horizontal position, which is the track an overhead marker would give. The bench does not render an ArUco image; the horizontal coordinate is read from the body pose. Finite amplitude and about 4 mm of servo sag leave the exponent at 0.495 and g about 1.3% low, inside both bars.
- The incline block is a slide joint along the ramp. Friction loss is set to μ m g cos θ with μ = 0.1, which is the Coulomb force from that coefficient. A free box on a contact ramp lost contact at the steeper angles and did not recover μ within 0.05, so the plant uses the joint friction the simulator actually integrates. Acceleration is a quadratic fit of the tracked position.
- E3 searches image distance by minimizing the geometric blur circle from physlab.optics.paraxial. The focal length is hidden from the search. The conjugate is the thin-lens image distance.
- uv sync --all-extras --dev was not re-run. T1–T8 already recorded that it fails because pyaudio needs portaudio.h. uv sync --dev is the working sync.
